  A columns slide divides its body at a thematic break, --- on a line of its
  own. What is above the break is the left column and what is below it is the
  right.

  Only a page style the theme names as splitting does this. In every other style
  a --- is a horizontal rule, and a second break in a splitting slide is
  reported as a problem against the slide.
